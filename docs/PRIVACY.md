# Privacy Policy for Lockdown Heist: Bandit

**Last updated:** October 2026

**Lockdown Heist: Bandit** is developed as a pure, privacy-first, offline arcade stealth game. We believe players should be able to enjoy video games without having their personal information gathered, tracked, or monetized.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection
- **We do not collect any personal data.**
- We do not ask for, gather, or store your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers.
- We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking frameworks.

## 2. No Internet Access
The game requires **zero network permissions** (`INTERNET` permission is disabled in the Android manifest). The game runs 100% offline and cannot communicate with any external server.

## 3. Local On-Device Storage
All game save data (such as cleared floors, collected coins, unlocked suits, and accessibility preferences) is stored locally on your device in the standard app-private storage directory (`user://save_data.json`). This data never leaves your device and is erased when the application is uninstalled.

## 4. Device Permissions
The game requests only one non-dangerous hardware feature:
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Used exclusively to provide subtle tactile feedback (haptic pulses) during gameplay actions such as knockouts and alert pulses. This feature can be turned off anytime in the in-game Settings menu.

The game never requests camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage access, or advertising IDs.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Because we collect no personal data whatsoever from any user, our game is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR, and Google Play Families Policy.
